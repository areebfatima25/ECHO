4. Physical Interpretation & Defense
   
The experimental findings resolve the quantum mystery of Project ECHO.

In Module 03, measuring entangled pairs produces correlated outcomes in any matched basis, distinguishing quantum entanglement from pre-shared classical variables. However, because individual measurement outcomes at each detector are intrinsically random (50% probability of 0 or 1), local measurements alone convey zero signal.

In Module 06, Bob's receiver accuracy drops to 50% (pure random noise) when classical feed-forward bits are omitted9. Without classical correction bits, Bob's target qubit remains in an uncorrected, maximally mixed state. Because sending classical bits is bounded by the speed of light, quantum teleportation strictly complies with special relativity and the No-Communication Theorem.
